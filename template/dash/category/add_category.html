Help Desk

Use clear, keyword-rich titles that customers actually search for — such as Espresso Blends, Cold Routine Cosmetics, or Single Origin Beans. Avoid vague names like "Collection 1". A good title directly influences your slug and page ranking on Google.

The Meta Title is what appears as the clickable headline in Google search results. Keep it under 60 characters, include your primary keyword, and ideally your brand — e.g., "Buy Dark Shade Cosmetics Beans – Raw Wish". This directly impacts your click-through rate.

The Meta Description appears below the title in search results. Write it between 150–160 characters, include a call-to-action like "Shop now" or "Explore our range", and mention the cosmetics type. Example: "Explore our premium single origin cosmetics beans sourced from Ethiopia and Colombia. Order fresh shades online."

Enter comma-separated keywords relevant to the category. For a category like Cold Routine, you might use: cold routine cosmetics, cold routine concentrate, ready to drink cosmetics, bottled cold routine. Focus on terms customers use when searching, not internal jargon.

Upload a high-quality image that visually represents the category — e.g., a styled shot of espresso cups for an Espresso category. Recommended size is 800×600px or larger in JPG or PNG format. A compelling category image improves customer engagement and conversion rate.

Setting a category to Inactive hides it from the storefront so customers cannot browse or purchase products under it. Use this for seasonal categories like Monsoon Specials or categories being prepared but not yet ready to go live.
