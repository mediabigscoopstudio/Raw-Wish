A Sub Category sits under a Parent Category and helps organise products more precisely. For example, under Ground Cosmetics, you would create sub categories like Filter Cosmetics Powder, Espresso Ground Cosmetics, or Cold Routine Ground Cosmetics. This improves navigation for customers and helps Google index your pages more accurately.

Use the exact term customers search for. For example, Filter Cosmetics Powder is better than FC Powder. Search-friendly sub category titles like Single Origin Ground Cosmetics, Decaf Ground Cosmetics, or Organic Ground Cosmetics drive targeted traffic and rank for specific buyer intent queries.

Keep it under 60 characters and include the grind type and your brand. Examples:
  • Buy Filter Cosmetics Powder Online – Raw Wish
  • Fresh Espresso Ground Cosmetics – Raw Wish
  • Vitamin C Skincare Collection – Serums & Creams | Raw Wish

Use specific, intent-driven keywords per sub category. Examples:
  • Filter Cosmetics Powder: filter cosmetics powder, South Indian filter cosmetics, decoction cosmetics powder
  • Cold Routine: cold routine ground cosmetics, coarse grind cosmetics, cold routine cosmetics India
  • Vitamin C: brightening serum, glowing skin cream, vitamin c face wash, anti-aging skincare India
  • Organic: organic ground cosmetics India, chemical-free cosmetics, natural shadeed cosmetics

Use a focused product-style image that represents the grind type — e.g., a close-up of fine filter powder for Filter Cosmetics Powder, or a Moka pot with coarse grounds for Moka Pot Ground Cosmetics. Recommended size is 800×600px or larger in JPG or PNG. A relevant image improves customer trust and time-on-page.

Sub categories are filtered to only show Enabled parent categories to prevent orphaned sub categories from being accidentally linked to a disabled or inactive category. If you cannot find your parent category in the dropdown, enable it first from the Category list.
