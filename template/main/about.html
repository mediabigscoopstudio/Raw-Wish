Our Story

Beauty without
compromise.

We believe that the products you use every day should do more than just make you look good—they should make you feel powerful, grounded, and cared for.

The Origin

It started with a
simple question.

Why should we have to choose between clean ingredients and high-performance beauty? The answer was simple: we shouldn't.

Rawwish was born out of a frustration with an industry that often asks women to compromise. We wanted products that felt luxurious, performed flawlessly, and were formulated with ingredients we could actually trust.

Our journey began with months of research, working alongside leading cosmetic chemists to harness the power of potent botanicals—like Shea Butter, Rose, and Aloe Vera—without sacrificing the rich pigments and textures that make makeup a joy to wear.

Our Values

What we stand for.

Pure Ingredients

We source the highest quality natural extracts and oils. If an ingredient doesn't serve a purpose or benefit your skin, it doesn't make the cut.

High Performance

Clean doesn't mean compromising on quality. Our formulas are meticulously tested to ensure rich color payoff, long wear, and luxurious textures.

Conscious Creation

From ethically sourced botanicals to minimalist, recyclable packaging, we consider our impact on the planet at every stage of production.
