Coffee Seed Extract

Rich in antioxidants and caffeine, our cold-pressed coffee seed extract is a powerhouse for waking up tired skin. It helps to stimulate blood flow, reducing the appearance of puffiness and dark circles while defending against environmental stressors.

  • Stimulates microcirculation
  • Potent antioxidant protection
  • Depuffs and brightens
Sourced for moisture

Raw Shea Butter

The ultimate skin superfood. We use unrefined, ethical Shea Butter packed with vitamins A, E, and F. It deeply moisturizes and provides the creamy, blendable texture found in our lip colors and blushes without clogging pores.

  • Deeply hydrating and restorative
  • Rich in essential fatty acids
  • Creates a smooth, buttery texture
Sourced for soothing

Aloe Vera Leaf Juice

Revered for centuries for its healing properties, our organic Aloe Vera acts as a cooling, hydrating base for our liquid formulations. It binds moisture to the skin and immediately calms redness and irritation.

  • Calms and soothes sensitive skin
  • Weightless, non-greasy hydration
  • Promotes natural skin healing

A golden miracle worker. We harness the active compound Curcumin to dramatically brighten uneven skin tones and impart a natural, lit-from-within glow while fighting inflammation at the cellular level.

  • Brightens and evens skin tone
  • Powerful anti-inflammatory
  • Natural glow-booster
