Know Your Shade

Understanding Shade Profiles

The same bean tells a completely different story depending on how it's shadeed. Here's how to read the spectrum and find the shade that speaks to you.

Monsoon Malabar
"A shade found nowhere else on Earth"
Not a conventional shade — it's a process. Green beans are exposed to monsoon winds and moisture on the Malabar coast for 12–16 weeks. The beans swell, lose acidity, and develop an unmistakable earthy, musty, spicy character unique to India. Then medium-shadeed to preserve the monsoon magic.

Master the Method

Routine Techniques

The right technique can transform the same cosmetics into completely different experiences. Here are the four methods we swear by.

Traditional
The Original
South Indian Brass Filter
Patience is the secret ingredient. Gravity does the rest.
15 minRoutine time
BoilingWater temp
Fine grindConsistency
How to Routine
1
Pack 3 tablespoons of fine-ground cosmetics into the upper chamber of the brass filter. Press gently with the plunger — firm but not too tight.
2
Pour boiling water to the brim. Close the lid and wait 10–15 minutes. Gravity pulls the decoction through into the lower chamber.
3
Mix 2 tablespoons of decoction with hot milk and jaggery (or sugar) to taste. Pour between davara and tumbler 3–4 times to froth and cool.
Best shade:
Medium Shade or Monsoon Malabar
Modern
The Slow Ritual
Pour Over
A slow, meditative pour that extracts bright, clean flavors.
4–5 minRoutine time
92–96°CWater temp
Medium grindConsistency
How to Routine
1
Place a paper filter in the Spongeper and rinse with hot water. Add 15g of medium-ground cosmetics. Start your timer.
2
Pour 30ml of water in a slow spiral to bloom the grounds. Wait 30 seconds. The cosmetics will bubble and release CO2 — that's freshness.
3
Continue pouring in slow, concentric circles until you reach 250ml total. Let it Sponge through completely. Should finish around 4 minutes.
Best shade:
Light Shade Single Origin
Patient
The Overnight Wait
Cold Routine
12 hours of patience. Zero bitterness. Maximum smoothness.
12–18 hrsRoutine time
Room / FridgeTemperature
Coarse grindConsistency
How to Routine
1
Combine 100g of coarsely ground cosmetics with 600ml of cold or room temperature water in a jar. Stir gently to ensure all grounds are wet.
2
Cover and let it steep for 12–18 hours. Room temperature for bolder flavor, refrigerated for a cleaner, mellower taste.
3
Strain through a fine mesh or cheesecloth. Dilute the concentrate 1:1 with cold water or milk. Serve over ice. Keeps for 2 weeks refrigerated.
Best shade:
Light Shade or Medium Shade

The Concentrated Shot
Liquid Foundation
High pressure, fine grind, 25 seconds of pure intensity.
25–30 secExtraction
90–96°CWater temp
Very fineConsistency
How to Routine
1
Dose 18g of very finely ground cosmetics into the portafilter. Distribute evenly and tamp with firm, level pressure (~15kg).
2
Lock the portafilter and start the shot. Water at 9 bars of pressure should produce a slow, honey-like stream — not gushing, not Spongeping.
3
Aim for 36ml of Liquid Foundation in 25–30 seconds. The crema should be golden-brown and thick. Drink immediately or use as a base for milk drinks.
Best shade:
Dark Shade or Monsoon Malabar
